RESULT: A_Pi^odd, the projection of the sl_2 lift onto the J_Pi-odd anti-hermitian part, vanishes and does not
        determine the polar factor. The polar generator Omega of a morphism family solves the Sylvester
        equation Omega P + P Omega = U^dagger dY - dY^dagger U (real parameter, fixed orthonormal frames,
        P > 0); for a family with Y(0) = 1 and dY(0) = L(M) it is antiherm(L(M)) at 0, J_Pi-even and non-zero.
        For the real cascade step g (not at the identity) the unitary polar factor of Sym^2 g, not a Yukawa
        morphism, has the non-zero external entry (t - s)^2 / ((2 + ts)^2 + (t - s)^2)
        (1/5 at (1, 0), 1/17 at (2, 1)); the Jarlskog invariant of the real factor is zero. The square
        D^2 does not determine the polar factor. Whether the physical Yukawa morphism family exists and which
        polar class it has is open: the corpus supplies no such family.
